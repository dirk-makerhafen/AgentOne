What filing does
A filed proposal becomes a backlog work item with the full spec, acceptance check and evidence. Backlog items do not dispatch. A human promotes one to ready after reviewing it, and implementation still goes through the normal coding/verification path.

This page never applies a proposal, edits a manifest, or runs the memory walker. Duplicate fingerprints are shown once; the most specific scope wins.
