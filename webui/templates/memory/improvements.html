What filing does
A filed proposal becomes a backlog work item with the full spec, acceptance check and evidence. Backlog items do not dispatch; a human promotes one to ready after reviewing it. Duplicate fingerprints are shown once, the most specific scope wins, and this page never applies a proposal, edits a manifest, or runs the walker.
